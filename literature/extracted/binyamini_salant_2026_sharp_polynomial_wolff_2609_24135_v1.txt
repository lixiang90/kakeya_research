                                              ESTABLISHING THE POLYNOMIAL WOLFF AXIOMS FOR
                                                  δ-SEPARATED δ-TUBES WITH #O-MINIMALITY


                                                                        GAL BINYAMINI, YUVAL SALANT
arXiv:2609.24135v1 [math.CA] 21 Sep 2026




                                                    Abstract. We establish the full version of a conjecture of Guth and Zahl,
                                                    giving a lower bound for the volume of a semialgebraic set that has a large
                                                    intersection with a collection of δ-separated δ-tubes. Our proof uses o-minimal
                                                    methods to simplify the proof of Katz and Rogers, who proved the conjecture
                                                    up to a small factor. We also establish that the constants depend polynomially
                                                    on the complexity of the semialgebraic set, and more generally in the #o-
                                                    minimal setting.




                                                                                1. Introduction
                                           1.1. Main Results. In [5], Katz and Rogers confirm a near-optimal version of the
                                           following conjecture made by Guth and Zahl (see [3, Conjecture 1.2]). We say that
                                           a collection of δ-tubes (δ-neighborhoods of unit segments) in Rn is δ-separated if
                                           the tubes point in δ-separated directions. That is, the angle between the central
                                           lines of any two distinct tubes is at least δ. We also say that a semialgebraic set in
                                           Rn has complexity (at most) E if it can be defined using intersections and unions
                                           of polynomial inequalities in n variables whose sum of degrees is at most E.
                                           Conjecture 1.1.1 (δ-Separation Implies the Polynomial Wolff Axioms). Let 1 ≥
                                           λ ≥ δ > 0, let T be a δ-separated collection of δ-tubes in Rn , and let S ⊆ Rn be a
                                           semialgebraic set of complexity E. Then
                                                                                                                        1
                                                      |S|Rn ≥ # {T ∈ T : |T ∩ S|Rn ≥ λ|T |Rn } · δ n−1 · λn ·                 ,
                                                                                                                     constn,E
                                           where |X|Rn is the Lebesgue measure of X, #A is the number of elements in A,
                                           and constn,E is a non-negative constant that depends only on n, E.
                                              Katz and Rogers proved this conjecture up to a factor of δ ε (that is, they showed
                                           that |S|Rn ≥ # {T ∈ T : |T ∩ S|Rn ≥ λ|T |Rn } · δ n−1+ε · λn · const1n,E,ε ). A generalized
                                           version of this conjecture was then used to establish partial results regarding the
                                           Kakeya conjecture (see, for example, [4]).
                                              In this paper, we follow the proof from [5] and a newer version of this proof,
                                           as found in the proof of [4, Theorem 1.4]. We use the more general language of
                                           o-minimality and #o-minimality (see §2.2), and by simplifying the arguments we
                                           prove the full version of the conjecture with a constant of poly1 (E) , where polyn (E)
                                                                                                              n
                                           is a polynomial in E when n is fixed; see §1.2.

Remark 1.1.2. For readers interested primarily in the original conjecture, it is
safe to restrict attention everywhere in this paper to semialgebraic sets, as these
indeed constitute an o-minimal and a #o-minimal structure; see [1, Example 1.5.1].
Briefly, in this case, the format of the set can be taken as the ambient dimension
and the degree can be taken as the sum of the degrees in a presentation of the
semialgebraic set using equalities and inequalities.
    We establish the following theorem:
Theorem 1 (Main theorem). Let 1 ≥ λ, δ > 0 and let S ∈ Rn be definable in an
o-minimal structure. Let T be a δ-separated collection of δ-tubes in Rn such that
                           ∀T ∈ T :     |T ∩ S|Rn ≥ λ|T |Rn .
Then
                                    1
                             |S|Rn ≥   #T · λn · δ n−1 .
                                  C(S)
Here, C(S) > 0 is a constant depending on S. If S is definable in a #o-minimal
structure with format F and degree E, then the constant C(S) can be taken to be
polyF (E).
    In [5], Katz and Rogers used the Wongkew Lemma [7, Main Theorem], in the case
where S is the intersection of a δ-neighborhood of a degree (at most) D algebraic
variety of dimension k with a ball of constant radius, to find an upper bound of
|S| ≤ polyn (D) · δ n−k . This allowed them to prove a second conjecture by Guth,
which asked for an upper bound of constn,D,ε ·δ 1−k−ε for the number of δ-separated
δ-tubes that can be contained in such a set S. The result in our paper improves
this bound by eliminating the ε and establishing a constant that is polynomial in
D when n is fixed. That is, by combining Theorem 1 with the same method as in
[5], we get the following result:
Corollary 2. Let 1 ≥ δ > 0 and let S ⊆ Rn be the intersection of the δ-
neighborhood of an algebraic variety of dimension k and degree at most D with
a closed ball of constant radius. Let T be a collection of δ-tubes that are contained
in S and point in δ-separated directions. Then
                                #T ≤ polyn (D) · δ 1−k .
1.2. Overview. We briefly address the two key points where our arguments differ
from [5], allowing us to avoid the additional δ ε term.
   In the first half of [5], the authors integrate over a semialgebraic set. This is done
in two steps: first, the Yomdin-Gromov algebraic lemma [5, Lemma 2.3] is used to
obtain a C r -parametrization. This map is then approximated by a polynomial,
allowing the authors to use the Bézout theorem to obtain an effective estimate.
In order for this approximation to be sufficiently accurate, it is necessary to use a
large r = θ( 1ε ) which translates into a constε δ ε error term. We work instead directly
with the relevant semialgebraic set, replacing the use of the Bézout theorem by more
general o-minimal (or #o-minimal) bounds, thus avoiding the approximation step
altogether.
   In the second half of [5], there is another step involving a dyadic pigeonhole
principle which introduces another log δ −1 error term. In the original argument,
this was absorbed into the δ ε error term, so there was little point in attempting
to improve this step. However, we show that a more refined geometric approach
          POLYNOMIAL WOLFF AXIOMS FOR δ-TUBES WITH #O-MINIMALITY                           3


can be used to eliminate this additional error term, thus recovering the full original
conjecture.
   It turns out that all of the arguments in the paper continue to hold in the more
general context of o-minimal structures (or, for polynomial bounds on constants,
#o-minimal structures), so we have chosen to present the results in this generality.

We present the arguments in the #o-minimal case, the o-minimal case being entirely
analogous.

                                   2. Preliminaries
2.1. Asymptotic growth. We use the notation consta1 ,...,ak to denote any quan-
tity that is bounded from above by a specific function that depends only on a1 , ..., ak
and polya1 ,...,ak (b1 , ..., bk ) to denote any quantity which, once a1 , ..., ak are fixed,
becomes bounded from above by a specific polynomial in b1 , ..., bk . All constants
and polynomials in this text can be made explicit in principle.
2.2. O-minimality and #o-minimality. We refer the reader to [6] for an intro-
duction to the theory of o-minimal structures, and to [1] for the theory of #o-minimal
structures. All of the o-minimal structures considered in this paper are taken over
the real numbers. We list below the main results needed for our paper, focusing
on the case of a fixed #o-minimal structure S. We briefly indicate the correspond-
ing statements in the o-minimal case. By [1, Remark 1.37], we may also assume
without loss of generality that S has sharp cell decomposition.
Theorem 2.2.1 (Definable Choice, [1, Proposition 3.1]). Let X ⊆ Ra × Rb be a
definable set of degree E and format F , and let Π : Ra × Rb → Rb be the projection
onto the last b coordinates. Then there exists a definable section f : Π(X) → Ra
of degree polyF (E) and format constF . That is, f satisfies (f (p), p) ∈ X for each
p ∈ Π(X).
  In the o-minimal case, the same result holds without the corresponding bounds
on the format and degree.
  For the next theorem, we will need the notion of cellular decomposition in #o-
minimality: see [1, §1.7] for the general definition and key properties.
Theorem 2.2.2 (C k cell decomposition). Let A be a definable set of degree EA
and format FA in a #o-minimal structure that has #CD, and let k ∈ N. Then A
can be partitioned into polyFA ,k (EA ) cells B1 , ..., Bl that are of degree polyFA ,k (EA )
and format constFA ,k , so that each Bi is a C k -embedding of an open cube (0, 1)di
via a definable map ψi of degree polyFA ,k (EA ) and format constFA ,k .
   Let f : A → Rb be a definable function of degree Ef and format Ff . Then
the cell decomposition of A can be chosen so that the map f ◦ ψi : (0, 1)di → Rb
is C k for each i. In this case, we will have polyFA ,Ff ,k (EA , Ef ) cells of degree
polyFA ,Ff ,k (EA , Ef ) and format constFA ,Ff ,k and the C k -embeddings will be of de-
gree polyFA ,Ff ,k (EA , Ef ) and format constFA ,Ff ,k .
  In the o-minimal case, the same result holds without the corresponding bounds
on the format and degree.

2.3. Notation and definitions. We introduce some notation and definitions that
we will use throughout. For A a measurable subset of Rk , we use |A|Rk for the
standard Lebesgue measure of A in Rk . For x ∈ Rk , we use ∥x∥Rk for the Euclidean
length of x.
Definition 2.3.1 (λ-segment). Let λ > 0, and let a, d ∈ Rm . We define the
λ-segment starting at (a, 0) with direction (d, 1) to be the line segment
                    la,d (λ) := {(a + t · d, t) : t ∈ [0, λ]} ⊆ Rm+1 .
Notation 2.3.2 (Projection Onto Directions). Let la,d (λ) be a λ-segment, and let
L be a collection of λ-segments. We define the projections
                                   Πdir (la,d (λ)) := d
and
                             Πdir (L) := {Πdir (l) : l ∈ L}.
Definition 2.3.3. Given x, y ∈ Rm , we define lx,y := {(x + t · y, t) : t ∈ R} ⊆ Rm+1
to be the (infinite) line passing through (x, 0) and pointing in the direction (y, 1).
Definition 2.3.4 (β × δ-tube). Let β > 0, δ > 0 and let a ∈ Rm , d ∈ [0, 1]m . We
define the β × δ-tube centered at (a, 0) with direction (d, 1) to be
            Ta,d (β, δ) := {(x, t) ∈ Rm × [0, β] : ∥x − (a + t · d)∥Rm ≤ δ}.
Definition 2.3.5 (δ-tube). Let δ > 0 and let l ⊆ Rn be a line segment of Eu-
clidean length 1. We define Tl (δ) ⊆ Rn , the δ-tube centered around l, to be the
δ-neighborhood of l.
