                                                   ON THE PROJECTIONS OF ALMOST AHLFORS REGULAR SETS

                                                                            TUOMAS ORPONEN AND KEVIN REN


                                                  A BSTRACT. We show that the "sharp Kaufman projection theorem" from 2023 is sharp in
                                                  the class of Ahlfors p1, δ ´ϵ q-regular sets. This is in contrast with a recent result of the first
                                                  author, which improves the projection theorem in the class of Ahlfors p1, Cq-regular sets.
