Maximal and ( m, ǫ )-Kakeya bounds over Z/N Z for general N
Manik Dhar ∗
Abstract
We derive Maximal Kakeya estimates for functions over Z/N Z proving the Maximal Kakeya
conjecture for Z/N Z for general N as stated by Hickman and Wright [ HW18]. The proof
involves using polynomial method and linear algebra techniques from [ Dha21, Ars21a, DD21]
and generalizing a probabilistic method argument from [ DD22]. As another application we give
lower bounds for the size of ( m,ǫ )-Kakeya sets over Z/N Z. Using these ideas we also give a
new, simpler, and direct proof for Maximal Kakeya bounds over ﬁnit e ﬁelds (which were ﬁrst
proven in [ EOT10]) with almost sharp constants.

Wolﬀ in [ Wol99] ﬁrst asked the question of lower bounding the sizes of Kakey a sets over ﬁnite
ﬁelds whose resolution might help in proving the Euclidean K akeya conjecture. Wolﬀ’s conjecture
was proven by Dvir in [ Dvi09] by proving a lower bound of qn/n ! for Kakeya sets in Fn
q . Using the
method of multiplicities and its extensions [

Ellenberg, Oberlin and Tao in [ EOT10] proposed studying the size of Kakeya sets over the rings
Z/p kZ and Fq[x]/⟨xk⟩. They were motivated by the fact that these rings have “scale s” and hence are
closer to the Euclidean version of the problem. Hickman and W right posed the Kakeya set problem
for Z/N Z with arbitrary N and considered connections between the problem and the Kake ya
conjecture over the p-adics in [ HW18]. Indeed, proving strong enough lower bounds for the sizes
of Kakeya sets over the rings Z/p kZ and Fq[x]/⟨xk⟩ will resolve the Minkowski dimension Kakeya
set conjecture for the p-adic integers and and the power series ring Fq[[x]] respectively [ EOT10,
DH13, HW18]. The Kakeya problem over these rings is interesting as simi larly to the Euclidean
case one can construct Kakeya sets of Haar measure 0 for the p-adic integers and the power series
ring Fq[[x]]. The constructions and generalizations to other setting s can be found in [ DH13, Fra16,
Car18, HW18, Dha21].
Forn = 2 and N =pk, Dummit and Hablicsek in [ DH13] proved that the sizes of Kakeya sets
are lower bounded by p2k/ 2k. The author and Dvir [ DD21] gave almost tight Kakeya set lower
bounds for Z/N Z with square-free N . Arsovski in [ Ars21b] gave the ﬁrst ( m,ǫ )-Kakeya set lower
bounds for Z/p kZ resolving the p-adic Kakeya conjecture. A quantitatively stronger bound f or
(m,ǫ )-Kakeya sets over Z/p kZ were proven in [ Dha21] (by extending the proof in [ Ars21a] which is
an earlier version of [ Ars21b] with a diﬀerent proof for only ( pk, 1)-Kakeya sets) and shown to be
almost sharp for ( pk, 1)-Kakeya sets. [ Dha21] also proved Kakeya set lower bounds over Z/N Z for

Given a matrix A with entries in F[z]/⟨f (z)⟩ we want to construct a new matrix with entries

F act 2.7 (Unique representation of elements in F[z]/⟨f (z)⟩). Let F be a ﬁeld and f (z) a non-
constant polynomial in F[z] of degree d >0. Every element in F[z]/⟨f (z)⟩ is uniquely represented

Deﬁnition 2.8 (Coeﬃcient matrix of A). Let F be a ﬁeld and f (z) a non-constant polynomial

dn1× n2 whose rows are labelled by elements in {0,...,d − 1}× [n1] such that its (i,j )’th row is
formed by the coeﬃcients of zi of the polynomial entries of the j’th row of A.
The key property about the coeﬃcient matrix immediately fol lows from its deﬁnition.
F act 2.9. Let F be a ﬁeld and f (z) a non-constant polynomial in F[z] of degree d > 0. Given
any matrix A with entries in F[z]/⟨f (z)⟩ and its coeﬃcient matrix Coeﬀ(A) it is the case that an
F-linear combination of a subset of columns of A is 0 if and only if the corresponding F-linear
combination of the same subset of columns of Coeﬀ(A) is also 0.
In particular, the F-rank of A equals the F-rank of Coeﬀ(A).
