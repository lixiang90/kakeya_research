Unions of lines in Rn
Joshua Zahl ∗
August 8, 2022
Abstract
We prove a conjecture of D. Oberlin on the dimension of unions of lines in Rn. If d ≥ 1 is an
integer, 0 ≤ β ≤ 1, and L is a set of lines in Rn with Hausdorﬀ dimension at least 2( d − 1) + β ,
then the union of the lines in L has Hausdorﬀ dimension at least d + β . Our proof combines
a reﬁned version of the multilinear Kakeya theorem by Carbery and V aldimarsson with the
multilinear → linear argument of Bourgain and Guth.
